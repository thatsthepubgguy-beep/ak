Key Terminology

Understanding Digital Menu Solutions

New to QR menus? Here are the key terms you'll encounter when working with us.

QR Code
A scannable 2D barcode customers point their phone camera at to instantly open your digital menu — no app download needed.
Digital Menu
A fully interactive, web-based version of your menu that opens on any smartphone. Update prices, items, and photos in real time without reprinting.
Multi-Language
Your menu displayed in multiple languages (e.g. English, Amharic) so every guest can browse in their preferred language.
Fasting / Non-Fasting Filter
A one-tap filter that shows only fasting-friendly (vegan/Orthodox) or non-fasting dishes — essential for Ethiopian hospitality businesses.
Custom Branding
Your logo, brand colors, and typography applied to your digital menu so it looks and feels like your business — not a generic template.
Free Updates
During your update period, we handle all menu changes, price edits, and new item additions — no extra fees, no waiting.
PWA (Progressive Web App)
Technology that lets your menu load even with poor or no internet connection, so guests always see your full menu regardless of signal quality.
One-Time Setup Fee
You pay once for your digital menu to be built and launched. There are no monthly subscriptions — just optional renewal for extended free updates.
